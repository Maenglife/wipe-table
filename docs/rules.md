# Wipe Table — one-page rules (v0)

A 2–4 player game about one night on a hostile survival island. One sitting, about 45 minutes. You are not simulating a video game: you are drafting a wipe, then playing the table that the randomizer dealt.

This is an unofficial fan ruleset. It is not affiliated with Facepunch.

## What you need

- The 10 wipe cards from **Wipe Table** (print them, use index cards, or leave the web table open)
- Resource tokens: **Wood, Stone, Metal, Sulfur, Cloth, Food, Scrap** (paper chits are fine)
- A **raid token** per player
- A **base mat** per player (a sheet of paper with slots for doors, cupboard, turret)
- A **blueprint strip** per player (cards from the wipe you have unlocked)

If a mix is raid-heavy (four or more Raid or Event cards), play to last base standing. Otherwise play to most Scrap when the wipe ends.

## Setup

1. Generate tonight's wipe. Those 10 cards are the only crafts, monuments, and events in play.
2. Each player starts with 2 Wood, 1 Stone, 1 Cloth, a Sleeping Bag slot (empty until crafted), and no blueprints.
3. Put the 10 cards in the center, grouped by set. Monuments are places you can visit. Events are round timers or ambushes. Survival cards are crafts you can buy into your deck as blueprints, then play onto your base.
4. Pick a starting player. Play proceeds clockwise.

## The wipe cards

The randomizer is the setup, not the board. During play:

- **Survival** cards are crafts and buildings. You must unlock a card as a **blueprint** before you can craft it onto your base (exception: cost 2–3 Survival cards may be crafted once without a blueprint, then they still need unlocking to repeat).
- **Monuments** are shared locations. On your turn you may visit one monument after gathering, paying its printed cost in time: you must spend resources or take the listed risk to take its reward.
- **Events** fire at the start of a round or when their text says so. Top-end events (cost 6+) are late-wipe pressure. If the table stalls on 5-cost crafts, the randomizer tries to include one of these.

Cards in the wipe that you have not unlocked are still on the island. You can raid a monument or contest an event without owning that card.

## A turn

Each turn has three steps. You must gather. You may craft. You may raid.

### 1. Gather

Draw 2 resources of your choice from Wood / Stone / Cloth. If you have a gather bonus in play (Bow, Hatchet, Recycler, a monument visit from last turn), add it now.

If an Event says a crate or patrol is in play, resolve its start-of-turn text before you draw.

### 2. Craft

Spend resources equal to a card's **cost** to:

- Unlock it as a blueprint (pay the cost, tuck a copy under your strip — you now own the recipe), or
- Play a blueprint you already own onto your base (pay the cost again). Buildings stay. Tools and explosives are spent when their text says so.

You may craft one card per turn unless a card says otherwise. Workbench Three, if in the wipe and unlocked, lets you unlock two extra blueprints when you play it.

### 3. Raid (optional)

Spend your raid token to attack:

- **Another player's wall.** Compare your raid (explosives, ammo, turret-suppression) to their highest door or High External Wall. If you beat it, take 2 Scrap from them and they lose that wall. If you fail, they keep the wall and you take 1 damage.
- **A monument in the wipe.** Pay the monument's raid clause (Airfield crate, Military Tunnels, Launch Site, and similar). Take the printed reward.

You regain your raid token at the start of your next turn unless an Event spent it.

**Damage:** 3 damage and you are downed. Discard 2 resources, return to your Sleeping Bag if you have one, otherwise respawn with 1 Wood. Repair damage by spending 1 Food during gather.

## Blueprints

Naked turns feel bad; gearing should feel earned.

- Unlocking a wipe card puts it on your blueprint strip. Later crafts of that card cost as printed, or 1 less if Workbench Three is on your base.
- You may buy a blueprint at **Outpost** without crafting it onto your base that turn.
- Tool Cupboard grants one free unlock when placed.
- You cannot craft a 4+ Survival card you have not unlocked.

## Winning

The wipe ends when any of these is true:

- A player has 12 Scrap, or
- Only one base still has a door or Tool Cupboard, or
- Eight rounds have passed (stack a stone each full rotation)

Then:

- **Raid-heavy mix** (4+ cards with type Raid or Event): last base standing wins. Scrap is the tie-breaker.
- **Otherwise:** most Scrap wins. Buildings are worth 1 Scrap each if you need a tie-break.

## Shorthand cost curve

Costs are the randomizer's language, not a second currency.

| Band | Cost | On the island |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early | 2–3 | Wood and stone: bags, bows, wooden doors |
| Mid | 4 | Metal: garage door, turret, recycler, cupboard |
| Late | 5 | Sulfur / HQM: charges, armored door, high walls |
| Top | 6+ | Wipe-end events: heli, cargo, oil, Bradley |

You do not have to use every card. The 10 cards are what *can* happen tonight.

## Table manners

Count Scrap in the open. When an Event is loud (Bradley, helicopter, signal), every player may join. If a card and this sheet disagree, the card wins. If two cards disagree, the current player decides, then the group writes a house note.

That is the whole v0 ruleset. Generate a wipe, deal the ten, and play until the island is quiet.
