HOW TO PLAY

The wipe is the game.

Current game: solo expedition — building and a player-triggered extraction, no combat. Open Expedition. Design note: docs/EXPEDITION.md.

Legacy tabletop: 2–4 players from docs/rules.md, or the 2-player hotseat from Hotseat this wipe. Those modes still deal 10 cards as crafts, monuments, and events. Thin hotseat: docs/PLAYABLE.md.

Expedition turn

  1. Move or recall — walk adjacent zones, or return to a claimed bed.
  2. Gather / build / craft — 1×1 → 1×2 → 2×2, then install what you discovered.
  3. Search — monuments and the wreck are scavenged, never fought.
  4. Extract — assemble the skiff on Wreck Beach when the parts are in.

Legacy hotseat turn

  1. Gather — draw 2 resources (Wood / Stone / Cloth), plus any tool bonuses.
  2. Craft — unlock a wipe card as a blueprint, or play a blueprint onto your base for its cost.
  3. Raid (optional) — hit another player's wall, or a monument printed in the wipe.

Blueprints

In expedition, finds come from searching monuments. In the legacy sheet, unlocking a card tucks it onto your strip. Cost 2–3 Survival cards may be crafted once without a blueprint.

Winning

Expedition ends when you choose to leave. Legacy hotseat: most Scrap at 12, last base standing, or eight rounds — raid-heavy mixes prefer last base standing.

Cost bands (legacy randomizer)

2–3 wood/stone · 4 metal · 5 sulfur/HQM · 6+ wipe-end events (Bradley, cargo, oil, heli). Balanced mode is a house curve, not an official rule.
