    This is a compact solo game. There is no raid step and no PvP. Full design note: docs/EXPEDITION.md.
    
      
    Each wipe changes the build
    
      
      
        
    • Island — three resource layouts on one zone graph.
      • 
        
    • Discoveries — four blueprints from a pool of six. The layout always includes its featured station.
      • 
        
    • Opportunity — repair the extraction skiff and choose when to leave.
      • 
      
    
      
    A day
    
      
    Four actions: walk, gather, build, craft, install, search, recall, assemble, or extract. Searching monuments is scavenging, not a fight.
    
      
    The skiff
    
      
    Visit Wreck Beach to identify it. Pontoon plate from Train Yard, starter coil from Military Tunnels, fuel kit crafted at a claimed site (1 metal or scrap + 1 wood, or a slower raw pile). Assemble on the beach, then extract.
    
      
    Demo seeds
    
      
    wipe-1 is Harbor scrap (compact recycler). wipe-4 is Inland ore (furnace line). wipe-0 is Long route (outpost).
