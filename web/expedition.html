    A day
    
      
    Four actions, all from the island: click adjacent ground to walk, click HERE to gather/search/build on that tile, click a claimed bed to recall, click the wreck skiff to extract when it is ready. Searching monuments is scavenging, not a fight.
    
      
    The skiff
    
      
    It sits on Wreck Beach. Identify it there. Pontoon plate from Train Yard, starter coil from Military Tunnels, fuel kit crafted at a claimed site (1 metal or scrap + 1 wood, or a slower raw pile). Assemble on the beach, then click the skiff to extract.
