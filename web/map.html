    MAP RULES

    Two actions. One island.

    2 players, same browser. Full sheet: docs/MAP-PLAYABLE.md. Cards here are loot and events, not a 10-card kingdom.

    Turn

    Take 2 actions: move one road, scavenge (up to 2, carry 5), deposit at your base, build a cupboard on a clearing (2 wood + 1 stone), craft at your base, visit a monument, draw one card, or play a card.

    Progression

    Beaches start at Workbench 0. Visit Outpost for WB1, Airfield for WB2, Launch Site for WB3. Pay 1 food or 2 resources.

    Fight

    Ending a move on the other player starts a fight. Attack 1 + gear + d6. Defense 1 + armor + door if you fight on their base + d6. Winner takes 2 resources (or 1 scrap). Loser takes a wound; 3 wounds is a down.

    Win

    First to 12 scrap, or most scrap after 10 rounds. Three downs with the other player still holding a base also ends it.
